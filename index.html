Quick Reference

Capture from your phone

Share sheet or Home Screen → Capture shortcut. First option is Just capture — no filing decision needed; it lands in Inbox flagged unsorted.

Or file directly as Task, Deadline, Read, Garden, Spark, or Recipe. Files sync via Dropbox and route within ~3 min.

Sparks & Recipes

Spark = a link + your one-sentence reaction (permanent commonplace log).

Recipe = paste a link (or share screenshots); scrapeable pages become full recipes, Instagram/Threads become bookmarks. Use Plan This Week for a grocery list.
